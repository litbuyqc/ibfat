03 / The quick answer

What is an
Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list records the product, seller, available options, listed price and date checked.

Think of it as a research starting point. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability. Open the actual listing, check the selected variant and confirm the terms before buying.

06 / Before you buy

Clear answers.
Better decisions.

A short reference for spreadsheet shopping, sizing and product checks.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list records the product, seller, available options, listed price and date checked. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

Is this an official Oopbuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent HTML shopping guide, not an official Oopbuy service or a downloadable spreadsheet. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS and the displayed category destinations.

How should I check sizing before ordering?

Compare the seller's measurements with a similar item you already own. For clothing, check chest width, length, shoulders and sleeve length. For shoes, compare the stated foot-length or insole measurements and confirm how they were measured.

What should I look for in QC photos?

Compare the photographed color, size label, item shape, stitching and visible condition with the selected listing. Ask for measurements or close-ups when needed. QC photos can reveal visible differences but cannot establish authenticity or guarantee durability.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add the product price, domestic delivery, any service or payment fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Treat the result as an estimate until the seller or service confirms the package details and charges.

Are the pictured branded items confirmed CAFANS stock?

No. The images are visual references, not verified stock listings or authentication evidence. Check the destination listing for the actual item, seller, availability, product details and return terms.
